The product gap

Your ring lets you log it.
It just doesn't listen.

What a tag does today

  • Records that you took something
  • Shows up in Trends over time
  • Helps you notice a pattern yourself

What a tag doesn't do

  • Change tonight's Readiness score
  • Adjust your personal baseline
  • Tell you the number is wrong

No. Same ring, same sensors. This is a software change to how your baseline is calculated.

Readiness already scores you against your own rolling baseline, not against other people. Right now that baseline mixes your medicated days and your off days together, so you are being compared to a version of you that does not exist. Log a dose and today gets compared to your medicated baseline instead. It is not adding points. It is changing the comparison, which means the score can still be low when the night was genuinely bad.

Stop logging it and your baseline returns to normal over the same window already used to personalize your scores.

The concept launches with stimulants because the effect is largest and most consistent. The same architecture extends to anything that moves heart rate, HRV or sleep.
